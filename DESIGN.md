# Bun'Q Burgers — Locked Design Direction v1.0

## Idea
Real burger photography is the hero. Make retro typography frame the product while the interface stays spacious and calm.

## Palette
- Background: flat cream #FFF4DE (no textures or gradients)
- Display: deep burgundy #681D27
- CTA: mustard yellow #F4B942
- Content: charcoal #24211F
No additional colors without a functional reason.

## Layout
One centerpiece burger, an expressive retro headline behind/around it that NEVER obscures the actual food, one short support line, one CTA. Large whitespace. Navigation limited to brand + key sections + contact. Footer understated. For mobile, preserve visible brand, burger and CTA without overflow.

## No-decoration list
No checkerboard motifs, torn-paper effects, circular seals/stamps, excessive badges, floating labels, hand-drawn arrows, icon benefit rows, grain/noise or paper texture, excessive shadows, parallax or heavy 3D. Every decoration must serve hierarchy or meaning.

## Assets / copy
Brand logo, menu and photography are expected from owner but not yet supplied. Placeholder imagery and text must be clearly marked mock content. No fake claims about ingredients, freshness, ratings, origin, promotions, prices or address. Copywriting subject to owner approval.

## Visual acceptance
Check screenshots on 320, 375, 768, and 1440px viewports. Hero's focal hierarchy clear within initial viewport; no overlapping text/image; single primary action; keyboard-visible focus, touch-friendly controls and readable text. Use Taste Skill for direction and Anti-Slop DURING for rejection checks. Report results rather than declaring success without browser evidence.
